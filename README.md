# Red Brick Community Kitchen Recipe Scaler

## The Client and the Problem

Red Brick community kitchen needs a way to calculate proper serving amounts and costs to prevent food waste.

## Why It Matters
Preventing food waste is a good way to feed as many people as possible for the cheapest costs. 

## What the Tool Does
My recipe scaling tool can be used to properly determine the exact amounts needed for serving anywhere from 1-150 people.
It will soon be able to fully calculate cost for each serving as well.
## About the Data
The recipes are fictional examples I used from the prompt and made up. There are 5. They are: Chili, Cornbread, Vegetable Soup, Macaroni and Cheese,  and Rice and Beans.
